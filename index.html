Welcome

Welcome to ICFCIT-2027

Welcome to the official website of the International Conference on Future Computing and Intelligent Technologies (ICFCIT-2027), to be held on 21st–23rd September 2027 at SRM Institute of Science and Technology, Chennai, India.

ICFCIT-2027 brings together researchers, academicians, scientists, industry professionals, innovators, and students from around the world to share knowledge, present original research, and discuss emerging advancements in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, Data Science, Cybersecurity, Internet of Things, Computational Intelligence, Smart Systems, Communication Technologies, and Advanced Computing.

The conference provides an international platform for research presentations, technical discussions, interdisciplinary collaboration, and innovation, while fostering academia–industry partnerships and contributing to the development of secure, sustainable, intelligent, and human-centric technologies for future digital societies.

Purpose & Vision

Conference Objective

An International Platform

The objective of ICFCIT-2027 is to provide an international platform for researchers, academicians, scientists, industry professionals, innovators, and students to present recent advancements in future computing and intelligent technologies, exchange technical knowledge, and discuss emerging challenges and opportunities across multidisciplinary computing and engineering domains. The conference aims to strengthen interaction between academia and industry while encouraging meaningful technical discussions, knowledge sharing, and innovation.

Interdisciplinary Innovation

ICFCIT-2027 also aims to promote interdisciplinary research, innovative thinking, and collaboration in emerging areas such as Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, Data Science and Big Data Analytics, Cybersecurity, Internet of Things, Computational Intelligence, Smart Systems, Communication Technologies, Edge and Cloud Computing, 5G/6G, and advanced computing applications. The conference will encourage the development of secure, scalable, sustainable, trustworthy, and human-centric technologies, facilitate research collaborations and industry partnerships, and provide researchers and students with opportunities to showcase original research and contribute to innovative solutions addressing real-world challenges.
