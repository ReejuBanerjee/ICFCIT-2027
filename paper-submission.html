Important Note

  • Registration fees do not include accommodation, food, or travel expenses. Participants are advised to make their own arrangements.

Cancellation Policy

In the event of cancellation of registration by an author or participant, the registration fee paid will not be fully refunded. Any eligible refund will be processed only after deducting applicable administrative charges and bank transaction charges, which must be borne by the registrant. Please note that no refund will be provided once the paper has been scheduled for presentation in the conference technical sessions. Authors and participants are therefore requested to complete the registration process carefully before making the payment.

Author Conduct

DOs and DON’Ts

Follow these instructions closely to keep your submission and registration on track.

DOs

  • Submit only original and unpublished research papers related to the conference themes and tracks.
  • Ensure that the manuscript is prepared ethically and reflects genuine research work carried out by the authors.
  • Submit the paper in .doc / .docx format by using the official Sample Paper Template available on the conference website.
  • Properly cite all references, figures, tables, and datasets used in the paper.
  • Check the plagiarism percentage before submission and make sure the manuscript follows the conference plagiarism policy.
  • Carefully read all conference guidelines, rules, and important instructions before submitting the paper.
  • Keep track of important dates from the conference website and submit the paper before the deadline.
  • Complete the registration process within the specified registration period if the paper is accepted.
  • Use only the official conference website and official conference email ID for communication and updates.
  • Regularly check your registered email ID and mobile number for conference notifications and updates.
  • Request the official Conference Schedule from the conference coordinator only after the registration process is completed.

× DON’Ts

  • Do not submit papers containing high plagiarism, copied content, or manipulated research data. Such papers will be rejected immediately.
  • Do not submit research articles fully generated using Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools without proper author contribution, verification, and validation.
  • Do not include fake references, fabricated results, manipulated figures, or misleading experimental outcomes in the manuscript.
  • Do not submit papers that are already published or under review in any other conference or journal.
  • Do not use inappropriate, offensive, political, religious, or controversial images and content in the paper or presentation.
  • Do not book travel or accommodation before receiving the official Conference Schedule email from the conference coordinator. The organizers will not be responsible for any financial loss due to schedule changes.
  • Do not arrive at the venue much earlier than the reporting time mentioned in the conference schedule.
  • Do not treat the payment acknowledgment from the payment gateway as the final confirmation of registration.
  • Wait for the official Conference Schedule email after successful registration and final confirmation from the conference team.
